# Substation Transformer SOP
For main step-up transformers in wind and solar pooling substations, trigger immediate inspection if Oil Temperature Indicator (OTI) exceeds 80C or Winding Temperature Indicator (WTI) exceeds 90C. For minor Buchholz relay gas alarm, collect gas sample for Dissolved Gas Analysis (DGA) within 2 hours. If major Buchholz trip or pressure relief valve operates, immediately isolate high-voltage breakers and do not re-energize without insulation resistance and breakdown voltage testing.
