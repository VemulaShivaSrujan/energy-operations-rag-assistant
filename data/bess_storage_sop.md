# BESS Energy Storage SOP
For Battery Energy Storage Systems (BESS), maintain State of Charge (SOC) between 15% and 90% during cyclic dispatch. If battery module cell temperature exceeds 45C, verify HVAC chiller operation and reduce charge/discharge C-rate. If cell temperature exceeds 55C or off-gas detection triggers, execute emergency trip, isolate DC racks, activate aerosol fire suppression interlocks, and evacuate the container enclosure.
